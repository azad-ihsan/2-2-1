About This Feature

Qazi Muhammad (1893–1947) was a Kurdish leader who established the Republic of Mahabad in 1946, the first Kurdish autonomous state. He is remembered as a symbol of Kurdish identity and unity.

The phrase "2 + 2 = 1" represents the idea that divided parts coming together create something greater—unity. In this context, it symbolizes the aspiration for Kurdish unity across borders.

"کوردستانم هەر ووڵاتێکەو نابێت بە چوار"

"My Kurdistan, a single homeland, shall never be four."
